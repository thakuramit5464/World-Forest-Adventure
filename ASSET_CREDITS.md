## Placeholder content

The houses, well, forest gate, Lookout Hill, fences, bench and supply crates are still built from Unity primitive meshes and flat-colour URP materials created by `Assets/_Game/Scripts/Editor/Phase1SceneBuilder.cs`. They contain no third-party art.

## Candidate free sources (not yet imported, licenses to be verified at import time)

- Buildings: a CC0 stylized village/medieval kit to replace the primitive houses, well and gate.
- Wind/sway: the MegaKit's stylized wind shaders ship only in the paid Source tier; a simple custom sway shader is an option.
- Environment: low-poly nature/village packs in a single consistent style.

## Generated content

Assets under `Assets/_Game/Generated/` (prefixed `Gen_`) are original, generated by this project's own editor code (`Assets/_Game/Scripts/Editor/AssetGenerator/`). They contain no third-party art and need no attribution.
