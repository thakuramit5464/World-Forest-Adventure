# Rural Courtyard House

A reusable building for *Trails of Greenhaven*, reproduced from the owner's `Rural Courtyard House Reference Sheet.png` (project root).

It is generated by C# in Unity: Blender is not installed, and no external models or packages are used.

## What's in it (matching the reference)

| Module (GameObject) | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Courtyard` | Tiled concrete courtyard (1.2 m tiles with grout lines), raised kerb, sloped ramp to the main door, low stone steps, grey stone platform |
| `Section_Terracotta` | Terracotta block: two wooden doors, a barred window, an open doorway and an open door into a green-painted room, a facade pier, a back door and window, a flat slab roof with a deep front overhang and parapet, a rooftop tank stand |
| `Staircase` | Solid concrete stair (12 steps) with a stepped underside, from the courtyard to the terracotta roof |
| `Section_Cream` | Cream main block: large opening, double-door opening with transom, small window, rear windows, overhanging flat slab with a low rim, small dark fittings under the slab |
| `Section_Teal` | Weathered pale-teal block (heavy grime at the base), side window, flat roof hidden behind the walls |
| `WaterBasin` | Open rectangular concrete trough, painted teal outside, with dark water |
| `BoundaryWall` | Terracotta pillar and low wall with a brick lattice (jaali) panel |
| `Interior` | Cot, low table, curtains behind the grilles |
| `Doors, Gates and Windows` | Every door, grille and window is its own object, pivoted at its hinge or bottom-left. Identical ones share one mesh. Includes the dark-red diagonal-lattice grille window, the double grille door (right leaf open) with transom, wooden doors (one open) and barred windows |

There are no roof tiles, decorative chimneys or other features absent from the reference.

- **Units and pivot:** metres, ground level at y = 0. The courtyard faces −Z (the front). The footprint is about 19 × 13 m.
- **Materials:** one per category in `Generated/House/Materials`:
  - `M_House_Paint`: painted walls, with weathering baked into the colour cells;
  - `M_House_Concrete`;
  - `M_House_Paving`;
  - `M_House_Metal`: gates and grilles;
  - `M_House_Wood`;
  - `M_House_Interior`;
  - `M_House_Water`.

  All of them share one point-filtered palette texture, so you can tint each category independently in the Inspector. Regenerating keeps your tweaks.
- **Colliders:** box colliders cover every solid wall piece, floor, step, roof, parapet, basin wall and closed door or window. The ramp uses a rotated box. Door and window openings are left clear, and the open leaves have no colliders, so the player can walk in through:
  - the open grille door into the cream room;
  - the open doorway and open door into the green room;
  - the stair up to the terracotta roof, which has parapets except at the landing.
- **Mobile budget:** about 10.5k triangles in total and 7 materials (one shared texture). All structure is marked static for batching. There are no lights and no transparency.

## Commands (Greenhaven ▸ House)

- **Generate Rural Courtyard House** writes the meshes, materials and prefab `Generated/House/Prefabs/RuralCourtyardHouse.prefab`.
  - It is deterministic: the same code gives the same house.
  - It confirms before replacing a generated prefab and refuses to touch files it didn't create.
- **Create House Test Scene** builds `Scenes/HouseTest.unity` with the house, the player, camera and HUD. It reports:
  - whether every entrance is clear for the player's capsule;
  - a walk test with the player's CharacterController settings into the cream room and up the stair onto the roof.
- **Capture House Preview** renders four views to `Generated/House/House_Preview.png`.
- **Export House to OBJ** writes `Exports/House/RuralCourtyardHouse.obj`, `.mtl` and `T_HousePalette.png`.
  - Each module and door is an OBJ group, with one material per category.
  - Import it into Blender with File ▸ Import ▸ Wavefront (.obj). Set the palette texture to *Closest* interpolation.
  - The project has no FBX/glTF exporter package, so OBJ is used to avoid adding a dependency.

## Editing

All dimensions live at the top of `RuralCourtyardHouse.cs`: levels, footprint lines and the stair. Each section has its own `Build…` method:
- **Openings** are `Opening(u0, u1, v0, v1)` entries: metres along the wall from its start, and up from the ground.
- **Doors, grilles and windows** are listed in `BuildFixtures`.
- **Colours** are set in `HousePalette` (`HouseKit.cs`).
- **Weathering** is set by each `Paint`'s stain height. The teal block is the heaviest.

Re-run **Generate** to apply changes. Scenes using the prefab update in place.

## Placing it

Drag `RuralCourtyardHouse.prefab` into any scene on fairly level ground, with the courtyard front facing the approach.

It is not placed in the village or Cloudpeak automatically, so existing scenes are untouched. It needs about 20 × 14 m of flat ground. The courtyard slab is 0.2 m high, so it steps up from the surrounding terrain.
